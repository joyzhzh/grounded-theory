# Research agenda

**State:** `FIRE ORDERS ISSUED 2026-09-02 — NOT EXECUTED` (see [START_HERE__20260902.md](START_HERE__20260902.md))
**Proposed program:** Grounded Theory
**Proposed missions:** `GT-M01`, `GT-M02`

## Why two missions

The program needs two different kinds of knowledge, governed by different
claims and stop rules.

`GT-M01` asks what a defensible grounded-theory-oriented workflow requires. It
is claim-bearing methods research and therefore uses the full Evidence-First
chain: retained manifestations, exact quotes and locators, first-ten audit,
independent adjudication, correction, synthesis, and release audit.

`GT-M02` asks what reusable public machinery already exists. It is a
coverage-sensitive discovery problem and therefore uses SOTA Repository Scout
in literal `high-recall-map` mode. Its result is a compact discovery bundle,
not admitted methodological evidence.

The missions may run independently. Integration is one-way at first:
`GT-M02` may provide named leads to `GT-M01`; `GT-M01` must inspect and admit
the underlying manifestations itself. A Scout score, README claim, star count,
or validator PASS cannot cross that boundary as truth.

## GT-M01 — Evidence-First grounded-theory methods

### Question

What evidence-backed methodological requirements and controls are necessary
for an AI-assisted system to support defensible grounded-theory-oriented
process analysis from digital traces and archival qualitative materials?

### Lane catalog

The sixteen lane identities `GT-M01-L01`–`GT-M01-L16` are registered once, in
[MISSION_PORTFOLIO.md](MISSION_PORTFOLIO.md). The executable lane fields,
source-ownership rules, rival/null duties, dependencies, and stop laws are in
[plans/GT-M01_LANE_CATALOG.md](plans/GT-M01_LANE_CATALOG.md). This agenda does
not restate them; a test fails if any document cites an unregistered identity.

### Deliverables

- `METHOD_EVIDENCE_MAP.md`
- `METHOD_PROFILE_DECISION.md`
- `OPERATIONAL_ANALYSIS_CONTRACT.md`
- `PILOT_RUBRIC.md`
- `GT_LITE_SPEC.md`
- claim-to-record matrix, limitations, and unresolved disputes

### Claim ceiling

The mission may establish only what current admitted sources support. It may
recommend a method profile and tool controls; it cannot certify that an AI
system “does grounded theory,” validate the focal empirical study, establish
saturation, or authorize paper claims.

## GT-M02 — grounded-theory methods and tooling SOTA Scout

### Question

What public repositories, packages, skills, schemas, templates, datasets,
evaluation artifacts, and adjacent tools provide reusable machinery for
grounded-theory or auditable theory-building from qualitative data?

### Scope distinction

This is narrower than the existing Scout campaign
`2026-07-28__theory-building`. That campaign is a zero-credit baseline for
deduplication, vocabulary, and open leads. `GT-M02` focuses on grounded theory,
qualitative process analysis, and AI-assisted qualitative research machinery.

### Machinery cells and retrieval frames

The twelve machinery cells `GT-M02-C01`–`GT-M02-C12` and eight retrieval
frames `GT-M02-F01`–`GT-M02-F08` are registered once, in
[MISSION_PORTFOLIO.md](MISSION_PORTFOLIO.md). The prospective Scout contract
that operationalizes them is
[plans/GT-M02_SOTA_REPOSITORY_SCOUT.md](plans/GT-M02_SOTA_REPOSITORY_SCOUT.md).
They are Scout scope cells and search frames, not Evidence-First worker lanes.

### Canonical output and landing

The working bundle stays in the authorized campaign workspace or external
vault. After validation and independent review, the exact accepted result lands
at:

```text

  campaigns/<run-date>__grounded-theory-methods-tooling/
```

The date is the actual scope-freeze/run date, not the Phase A planning date.

### Claim ceiling

The intended language is `high-coverage, effort-bounded map as of <date>` if
the required frames and working-plateau reduction support it. If the ceiling
arrives with positive yield, the result is `high-coverage, effort-bounded, not
saturated`. It may never claim completeness, exhaustiveness, acquisition
eligibility, successful execution, or methodological validity from public
metadata and documentation alone.

## Integration into the tool

Tool implementation follows research, not the reverse:

1. `GT-M01` and `GT-M02` may run in parallel after separate exact fire orders;
   neither is a prerequisite for starting the other.
2. `GT-M02` may nominate reusable components and failure leads with zero
   evidentiary credit.
3. `GT-M01` independently admits doctrine and any claim-bearing artifact
   evidence needed for method decisions.
4. The Operator freezes the method profile and tool specification only after
   both missions have landed or closed with explicit limitations, unless an
   exact architecture waiver records why one result is unnecessary.
5. A synthetic benchmark tests structural behavior and refusal boundaries.
6. An authorized real-data pilot runs in an external private study workspace.
7. Independent review determines whether another analytical cycle is warranted.

No mission is active until a later exact fire order names its root, pin,
authority, seats, and ceilings.
