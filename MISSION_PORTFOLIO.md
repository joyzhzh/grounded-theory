# Mission portfolio — Grounded Theory

**State:** `UNFIRED — PHASE A ARCHITECTURE DRAFT`
**Proposed program:** `GT-01`
**Mission count:** 2
**Fired missions:** 0

This portfolio turns the two research needs into separate missions with
different evidentiary authority. `GT-M01` has stable Evidence-First worker
lanes. `GT-M02` is one lean Scout campaign with scope cells and retrieval
frames, not a second evidence-admission lane system. None of these planning
identities is a worker assignment or permission to begin research.

## Portfolio logic

| Mission | Purpose | Method | Output authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| `GT-M01` | Establish the evidence-backed methodological workflow | Evidence-First Deep Research | May support a reviewed method decision after admission and adjudication |
| `GT-M02` | Map reusable public repositories and adjacent machinery | SOTA Repository Scout, literal `high-recall-map` | Discovery and coverage memory only |

The missions may be researched in parallel after separate fire orders. They
do not merge their ledgers. A lead from `GT-M02` receives zero evidentiary
credit in `GT-M01` until the underlying manifestation is independently
retained, inspected, and admitted under the Evidence-First protocol.

## Dependency and stop rules

1. The Operator may fire either mission separately, with separate roots,
   pins, producers, reviewers, ceilings, and stop rules.
2. `GT-M02` may nominate artifacts; it cannot validate methods or authorize
   installation, execution, adoption, or evidence admission.
3. Neither mission is a prerequisite for starting the other. Tool design
   remains provisional until `GT-M01` supports a declared method profile and
   `GT-M02` has either landed or closed with explicit limitations, unless an
   exact architecture waiver records why one result is unnecessary.
4. A synthetic structural benchmark precedes any real-data pilot.
5. Blocked, thin, inaccessible, capped, or positive-tail routes remain
   unknown. Neither mission may autonomously declare theoretical saturation.
